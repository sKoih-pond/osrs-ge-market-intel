Top ideas

    Definitions

    Net GP
    Net margin per item multiplied by the feasible quantity. Before any fees you would not see in game, and assuming the policy below fills.
    Buy at
    Volume-weighted average instant-sell price over the price window, rounded up to whole coins. Your buy offer.
    Sell at
    Volume-weighted average instant-buy price over the price window, rounded down to whole coins. Your sell target.
    Tax
    2% of the sell price per item, rounded down and capped at 5,000,000 GP per item. Items on the published exemption list pay none, and prices under 50 GP round to no tax.
    Net margin
    Sell at, minus tax, minus buy at, per item.
    Quantity
    The smallest of the item's buy limit, what 50M GP of capital buys, and 10% of the last 4 hours' instant-sell volume (so your offer is a small part of the market). The label says which one set it.
    Data grade
    Whether the prices behind the idea are sound. A means both sides traded in every hour of the last day, the last trade was within 10 minutes, and this hour's prices are within 5% of the item's usual level. B is usable with care. C is withheld, for example when a single stray trade moves the price far from its usual level. The thresholds come from measured price stability, not guesses: see how the Data grade was derived.
    Price window
    The closed hour (UTC) whose 5-minute trade data produced the prices. Nothing after it is used.

    Policy casual-v1: 4-hour entry window, 24-hour exit horizon. This is a screen, not advice: past prices do not guarantee fills.
